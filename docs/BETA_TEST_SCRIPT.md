# Ten-minute beta test

Use only after the release checklist's invitation blockers are cleared. Obtain consent to store limited usage counts, recommendations/decision history and submitted feedback. Explain that Sleeper access is read-only and optional AI/news/images are off in the candidate. Do not prime people with a tour.

## Message to tester

1. Open FanEdge.
2. Connect your Sleeper username and select your league.
3. Use it however you naturally would for ten minutes.
4. Do not ask Dylan how to use a feature unless you're stuck. Send feedback when something seems confusing, wrong or slow.

FanEdge does not set your lineup or make transactions. Check current player status and league availability before acting. Trade discovery is experimental, not a guarantee of fair value.

## Interview after use

- What did you do first?
- What confused you?
- Did anything surprise you?
- Did FanEdge tell you anything you didn't know?
- Was any recommendation bad? Which one, and why?
- What did you want to do that you couldn't?
- What would make you return?
- Which feature was most useful?
- Would you use this alongside Sleeper?

## Observer record (private, not in Git)

Record build, device/width, league scoring/slots/team count, start/end time, tasks attempted, where assistance was required, errors and verbatim consented feedback. Do not collect passwords/API keys. Distinguish first-run abandonment, misunderstood language, missing feature and unsupported football inference. Run the audit CLI for the specifically consented username/league, label all surfaced items with GOOD/DEFENSIBLE/QUESTIONABLE/BAD and reason, and record reviewer identity separately. Repeat across at least two independent owners/leagues before inviting the full 5–10-person group. Keep observed users separate from automation/operator validation in reports.
